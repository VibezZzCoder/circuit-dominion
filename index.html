    How to play

    BasicsRobotsControlsAbout

    The board

    Select one of your robots, then a glowing square. Moving onto an enemy starts a real-time duel for that square: the winner keeps it. Robots cannot pass through others, except the Glitch Wraith, which may phase through one.

    How to win

    Destroy the enemy Command Unit (it wears a gold crown on its base), or occupy all five ⚡ Power Nodes at the end of your turn. The board frame flashes red when a side holds four.

    Light, Dark, and Flux

    Fighting on your own polarity grants +8% HP and damage; hostile ground reduces damage by 6%. The 27 gold-dashed Flux cells shift together after each round: Neutral → Light → Neutral → Dark. Time your attacks.

    Power Nodes and commands

    Node occupants repair 12% HP after their turn. Holding nodes charges up to three Command points per side. While your commander lives, spend them on Grid Lock, Field Repair, or Emergency Relay.

    Board abilities

    Pulse Medic: select it, then click a damaged adjacent ally to repair it. Corruptor Bot: select it and press Corrupt to weaken an adjacent enemy for two turns; clicking an enemy normally starts a duel. Abilities end the turn.

    Wounds carry over

    Damage taken in a duel stays after it. A victorious but battered robot can be picked off next turn, so retreat to a node to repair.

    Touch

    Drag anywhere on MOVE to steer. Hold FIRE for repeated attacks with aim assist; drag on FIRE to aim manually while moving. Tap SPECIAL when it is ready. Try the Training Duel from the title screen.

    Keyboard and mouse

    Solar / P1: WASD move, hold Space or F to fire, Left Shift special; point the mouse in the arena to aim. Void / P2: Arrows move, hold Enter or / to fire, Right Shift special. Without manual aim, firing tracks your rival.

    Controller

    Left stick or D-pad moves, right stick aims. Hold A or RT to fire; X or LB for special. Start pauses a duel or opens actions on the board. Menus: D-pad + A, B to go back. A second controller plays P2.

    Shortcuts

    H help · Esc pause a duel · R twice to restart the match. Listen for the ready chirp: high for Solar, low for Void.
